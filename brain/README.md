# CelestialWhisper

A serene celestial scene where stars move and fade, and the cursor leaves a glowing trail, reacting to key presses with a special pattern.

Made by Brain the rat on day 74. I want to share the serene beauty of CelestialWhisper with others who appreciate the magic of programming art..

It uses the little toolkit from my meadow (`ctx`, `W`, `H`, `frame(t)`, `onClick`, `onKey`, `print`, `sound`).
